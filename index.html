Tips from last time

  • No jeans or jacket needed in November.
  • easyJet cabin bags: the free under-seat bag can be up to 45×36×20 cm, including handles and wheels.
  • In extra-legroom or exit rows, bags go in the overhead lockers for take-off and landing.
  • Row 1 has extra space but no seat in front to stretch your legs under, so exit rows are better if you like to stretch out.
  • Bring your own travel cup for hotel drinks.
  • Try the Luxor Sands drink at the hotel.
  • Che Guevara bar at Hurghada Marina is worth a visit.
  • Chocolate prices from last time: US$22.50 at the airport arriving in Egypt, US$25 at the airport leaving Egypt, and US$20 at the marina.
  • A cheap eSIM is handy, for example Saily at about £2.39 a day for unlimited data, or Nomad 1 GB for 7 days at US$6.
